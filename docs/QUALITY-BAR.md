# The bar: mexicat/pdoom-video

The reference for "amazing" is mexicat/pdoom-video (its treatment is docs/TREATMENT.md there). Match its level; never copy its look, motifs or shots (no orange
spark-on-a-fuse, no P(doom) counter, no smile mask, no loss curves, no plotter unicorn).

What makes it amazing, as principles to meet:
- Idea density. Each scene is a visual pun or transformation of the line, never a literal
  illustration. Things turn into other things on the beat.
- Something is always moving; big changes land on the beat; strong eases, holds, then snaps.
  Inside a scene there are sub-cuts, reframes and camera snaps, not one slow drift.
- The lyric is part of the image, integrated differently every scene (written by something, riding
  a surface, stamped, typed, masked), never a subtitle on top.
- One recurring hook slam whose look escalates each time ("BROOD OF VIPERS" here).
- Deadpan precision: hairline rules, tiny mono annotations and catalogue labels next to huge type,
  Swiss asymmetric layout, generous negative space.
- Restraint in colour: bloom only on the glow colours (acid green, fire). Crisp type, never haloed.
- Not slop: no purple/cyan neon, no Matrix rain, no glowing brains, no lens-flare soup, no particle
  nebulae. Our cyberpunk is grimy and industrial: concrete, rust, fluorescent tubes, sodium, fire.
